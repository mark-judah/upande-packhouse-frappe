Greenhouse is recorded on harvest, receive, shelf and discards. The order steps (allocated to dispatched) carry no greenhouse and stay unfiltered by it.

Stems through each step

One shared scale · bar length is the stem count

Each step is counted on the date of its own event, so a step can exceed the one before it: a window can pack, issue or dispatch stems that were harvested, received or shelved before it opened, and one order step can be dated a day apart from the next. “Farm used” says which farm a step is filtered by: stock steps use the physical farm, order steps use the order’s farm (Sales Order farm, else pick list farm; orders with no farm drop out while a farm or region filter is on).
